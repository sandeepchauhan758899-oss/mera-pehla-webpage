Global Warming: Ek Gambhir Samasya

Prithvi ke tapman mein hone wali gair-prakritik vriddhi ko Global Warming kehte hain.

Karan (Causes)

  • Pedon ki andhadhundh katayi.
  • Jivashm indhan (Fossil Fuels) ka badhta upyog.
  • Industrialization aur pollution.

Samadhan (Solutions)

Humein adhik se adhik ped lagane chahiye, plastic ka upyog kam karna chahiye aur renewable energy (Solar, Wind) ko apnana chahiye.
